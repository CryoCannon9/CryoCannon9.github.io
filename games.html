Front of the House

Simulation Game


Click Here to Play

Front of the House is a relaxing bakery simulator where you bake goods, manage inventory, and serve customers through a 2D UI.

Inspired by my job at an actual bakery, I first built this simulation game in one week in Twine. This second version, built with GameMaker, was created with the focus of improving my pixel art and UI/UX skills.

The Lost Time-traveler

Interactive Fiction

Click Here to Play

You wake up in a shipping container, consoles and screens lining the walls. You find a note informing you that you're a time-traveler - it's up to you to decide where to go.

This is a game/story I created as my final for Intermediate Interactive Design at UAlbany as a Junior. Throughout the game, the user is prompted with several decisions that change the ending of the story. I constructed the story around these decisions using narrative bits - nonsequential story beats and exposition - that relied on the context of the others to form a cohesive ending.

Rome's Mansion

2D Story RPG - In Progress

One day, Rome ventures into the winding halls of his own mind...

What if we could enter the mansion of our minds, becoming the master, taking control of all our emotions, desires, and fears? Rome's Mansion is a passion project of mine, focusing on bringing together the aspects of storytelling and game design I've gathered through my time at college. As you explore the realm of Rome's dreams, you'll fight enemies with the power of darkness, bless them with loving light, or take both paths at once: you decide the fate of Rome's Mansion.

Landon McConnell, UAlbany 2024
